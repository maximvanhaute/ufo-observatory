# Our opening sound

We use “UFO Landing Sound Effect (Royalty Free)” by Altered Sound Effects, supplied by the user on 7 November 2026.

- Source video is https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RYZPQ0xE6do.
- Creator is https://www.youtube.com/@alteredsoundeffects.
- YouTube metadata labels the video “Creative Commons Attribution license (reuse allowed)”.
- The source clip is approximately 14 seconds. We extracted its AAC audio stream into dist/assets/ufo-landing.m4a.
- We show the title, creator, source link, and uploader’s licence label in About.

We attempt the opening sound on page entry when sound is enabled. A saved mute preference takes precedence. Browsers may require a click before playing sound. In that case the first click starts the intro, and the header also offers a Play intro button. Visitors can stop or replay it, and the sound toggle stops all audio.

We play the opening sound once per page visit and pause it if the page is hidden. We suppress the short interface cues while the intro is playing. Later navigation within the app keeps the existing sound effects without restarting the intro.
